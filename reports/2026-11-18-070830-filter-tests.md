# Filter tests, 2026-11-18

Code 43f0869; research-app data fingerprint d09e16aa32e780ea; 23 tests; 10,000 resamples each.

**What this asks.** Did a registered strategy do better or worse when a condition held at the signal? Each trade's *excess* is its gross return minus the mean of every trade of the same length and entry in the window (2010-01-01 to 2026-11-13, Lead v3's development window). The *difference* is the mean excess of the trades where the condition held minus the rest, in basis points per trade, with a 90% interval and a two-sided p from resampling calendar quarters of trades. q corrects for every test in this run. 1993-2009 is computed once per test version and then read from `holdout_ledger.jsonl`, where the condition can be measured there.

**Registered 2026-11-18 before any was run** (`backtester/splits.py`): ideas 7 (a gap up at the next open uses up the bounce) and 8 (trend and volatility) from the research app's idea ledger, plus the VIX curve, a release inside the trade, and a volatility-matched baseline from the 2026-11-17 audit.

**How to read the labels.** *Supported difference* needs development p < 0.05, q < 0.10 and the same sign in 1993-2009 with p < 0.10. *No meaningful difference*: the interval rules out a difference as large as the strategy's own mean excess. *Insufficient evidence*: the interval is too wide to say. Few trades make most splits wide: with about 100 trades a difference has to be well over 1 point per trade to show. None of these labels changes a trade verdict; a filter worth using becomes a new registered strategy version.
